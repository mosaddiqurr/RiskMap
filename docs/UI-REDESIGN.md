# RiskMap UI redesign

RiskMap uses a dark navy and electric-blue visual system, self-hosted Manrope typography, and shared surfaces across Home, Analyze, Exposure Map, Report, and About. The header keeps the main navigation and Launch demo action. Theme and animation controls have been removed.

The landing page automatically animates its connected-particle background, flowing network signals, pulsing nodes, orbit markers, scroll reveals, and closing callout. Animation remains enabled without a saved preference or a toggle. Decorative work suspends while its section is outside the viewport or the browser tab is hidden.

The desktop opening section adapts to browser height. The full diagram, recommendation, footnote, headline, and action buttons fit together on standard laptop and desktop viewports. The diagram receives the space left by its compact header and footer rather than growing proportionally with its width. Narrow layouts retain readable stacked content.

Keyboard-accessible evidence tabs and the remediation preview use the fictional demo dataset. Supporting labels and body copy have larger sizes throughout the application. Printed reports retain their light paper layout.

Browser checks cover remediation, keyboard navigation, evidence/report accessibility, readable text, automatic animation, and complete first-view visibility at 1891×850, 1440×900, 1366×768, 1242×668, 1280×600, 1024×768, and 900×668.

Updated desktop screenshots are in [screenshots](screenshots/). The local preview uses [port 3100](http://127.0.0.1:3100/).
